The problem

Digital-arrest and fake-KYC scam calls target elderly and rural Indians in their own language. The victim only learns it was a scam after the money is gone. No tool listens during the call and warns them in Telugu.

Step 1 · Listen

The browser captures the speaker's sound at 16 kHz and cuts it into small 1024-byte frames, one every 32 ms. A small Node server holds the Gnani API key, so it never reaches the browser.

Step 2 · Prisma speech-to-text

The server streams frames to Prisma over a WebSocket. Prisma detects where each sentence ends and returns Telugu text for it.

“డిజిటల్ అరెస్ట్ చేశాం, కాల్ కట్ చేయకండి”

Step 3 · Risk engine

Each sentence is checked for scam signals. Every new signal raises the meter.

Claims to be CBIDigital arrestKeep it secretAsks for OTP
Step 4 · Timbre text-to-speech

When risk crosses 60 out of 100, Kavach speaks a calm Telugu warning on the same speaker:

ఇది మోసం. వెంటనే కాల్ కట్ చేయండి.

Step 5 · Family report

Verdict: LIKELY SCAM (100/100)
Signals: CBI claim, digital arrest, secrecy, OTP demand, urgency
A timestamped timeline of every sentence, for the family to read.
